## Materials
*   **Stimulus Set:** A total of 24 emotional images were selected from the Open Affective Standardized Image Set (OASIS).
*   **Image Characteristics:** Stimuli were selected based on normative valence ratings, consisting exclusively of "positive" and "negative" items. Neutral images were deliberately excluded to focus specifically on the intensity of emotional processing.
*   **Counterbalancing:** The stimuli were divided into two matched sets of 12 images (Set A and Set B), ensuring no significant difference in average normative valence or arousal across sets. This was implemented to prevent repetition effects across the L1 and L2 blocks.
*   **Apparatus & Software:** The experimental paradigm was programmed using the **jsPsych** behavioral library (v7.3) and hosted online via GitHub Pages. The software handles dynamic language routing, precise stimulus presentation timing, and interactive response collection.

## Procedure
*   **Duration:** The overall experimental session lasts approximately 10–15 minutes.
*   **Informed Consent:** Prior to commencing the study, participants are presented with a digital informed consent form detailing the study's scope, data privacy measures, and voluntary nature. 
*   **Language Background (Demographics):** Following consent, participants complete a pre-task demographic questionnaire to assess bilingual proficiency. Crucial metrics collected include designated native language (L1), English (L2) proficiency (using the standardized CEFR scale: A1–C2), Age of Acquisition (AoA) for the L2, alongside standard age and gender demographic data.
*   **Experimental Task:** The study utilizes a within-subjects, blocked design where each participant evaluates images in both their L1 and L2. The order of language blocks (L1 first vs. L2 first) is counterbalanced based on the participant's assigned ID number (e.g., Even IDs receive L1 first, Odd IDs receive L2 first).
    *   **Trial Structure:** On each of the 24 trials, participants are presented with a central fixation cross for 500 ms, immediately followed by the target image. 
    *   **Measurement:** 
        1.  *Quantitative (Intensity):* Participants rate the overall emotional intensity elicited by the image on a 7-point Likert scale (1 = Not at all, 7 = Extremely). 
        2.  *Qualitative (Expression):* Participants provide a brief, free-text description characterizing their emotional response. 
        Both instructions and participant responses are strictly confined to the target language assigned to the current block.
*   **Debriefing:** Upon completion of all 24 trials, data is automatically exported and participants are presented with a debriefing screen. This screen explicitly clarifies the specific goals of the research—investigating potential differences in emotional processing between L1 and L2—and provides researcher contact information.
